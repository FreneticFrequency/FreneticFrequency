Artist Bio

Jose, known as Frenetic Frequency, is a dark night psychedelic trance live act based in Fundão, Portugal. Active in music production since 2006, his sound is forged around relentless high-frequency pressure, aggressive rhythmic structures, and deep psychedelic atmospheres crafted for late-night dancefloors.

Blending controlled chaos with precise sound design, Frenetic Frequency creates intense, mind-altering sonic journeys that dissolve perception and drive listeners into heightened states of consciousness.

His performances are immersive, powerful, and uncompromising, exploring the darker edges of modern psychedelic trance.
